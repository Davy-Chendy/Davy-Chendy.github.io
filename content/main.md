<h2 id="about-me" class="section-title">About Me</h2>
<p class="lead">I am <strong>Deyu Chen</strong> (<span lang="zh-CN">陈德宇</span>), a master’s student in Software Engineering at <a href="https://www.scut.edu.cn/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">South China University of Technology</a>, supervised by <strong>Assoc. Prof. Qing Du</strong> and <strong>Prof. Mingkui Tan</strong>. I received my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from SCUT in 2024.</p>
<p>My research focuses on <strong>test-time intelligence</strong>: enabling pre-trained models to learn and evolve during deployment. I am interested in test-time learning, adaptation, and editing for vision and large language models, with the goal of making deep models safer and more reliable in changing real-world environments.</p>
<ul class="modern-list">
  <li><strong>Continual and collaborative adaptation.</strong> Reusing knowledge over time and sharing it across devices, including devices with limited compute.</li>
  <li><strong>Stable test-time learning.</strong> Understanding and preventing representation collapse under distribution shifts.</li>
  <li><strong>Adaptive foundation models.</strong> Exploring how pre-trained models can improve from deployment-time data across tasks and domains.</li>
</ul>
<div class="collab-callout">I welcome research discussions and collaborations on test-time intelligence and reliable machine learning. Reach me at <a href="mailto:davychen2001@gmail.com">davychen2001@gmail.com</a>.</div>

<h2 id="experience" class="section-title">Research Experience</h2>
<div class="exp-card">
  <div class="exp-header"><h3 class="exp-title">Degrade-Free Test-Time Model Adaptation</h3><div class="exp-date">May 2024 – Feb 2026</div></div>
  <div class="exp-org">Representation stability · Adapt in the Wild &amp; ZeroSiam</div>
  <div class="exp-desc">Investigated representation collapse and developed complementary approaches: feature redundancy and inequity regularization to suppress correlated, biased representations, and an asymmetric Siamese architecture to resist collapse. Improved accuracy by up to <strong>21.9%</strong> in the evaluated settings and strengthened stability under challenging test-time shifts.</div>
</div>
<div class="exp-card">
  <div class="exp-header"><h3 class="exp-title">Cross-Device Collaborative Test-Time Model Adaptation</h3><div class="exp-date">Nov 2023 – Sep 2024</div></div>
  <div class="exp-org">Collaborative lifelong adaptation · NeurIPS 2024</div>
  <div class="exp-desc">Designed a reprogramming-based learning scheme to reuse previously learned and cross-device knowledge while mitigating forgetting. Developed a training-free collaboration scheme for low-resource devices based on domain similarity. Achieved up to <strong>78.0× faster adaptation in sample efficiency</strong> with <strong>2.7% additional computation</strong> in the evaluated settings.</div>
</div>
